# References

Curated reading for the categorical foundations and actuarial mathematics
underlying this project.

Local PDFs may be kept in this directory for convenience; binary files
(`*.pdf`, `*.zip`) are excluded from the repository because the underlying
material is typically copyrighted. Obtain copies via the publisher or
preprint server.

## Categorical foundations of learning

- **Fong, Spivak, Tuyéras** — *Backprop as Functor: A compositional
  perspective on supervised learning*. 2017. arXiv:1711.10455.
- **Cruttwell, Gavranović, Ghani, Wilson, Zanasi** — *Categorical
  Foundations of Gradient-Based Learning*. 2021. arXiv:2103.01931.
- **Capucci, Gavranović, Hedges, Rischel** — *Towards Foundations of
  Categorical Cybernetics*. 2021. arXiv:2105.06332.

## Lenses, optics, and the Para construction

- **Clarke, Elkins, Gibbons, Loregian, Milewski, Pillmore, Román** —
  *Profunctor Optics, a Categorical Update*. 2020. arXiv:2001.07488.
- **Riley** — *Categories of Optics*. 2018. arXiv:1809.00738.

## Actuarial mathematics and credibility

- **Bühlmann, Gisler** — *A Course in Credibility Theory and its
  Applications*. Springer Universitext, 2005.
  ISBN 978-3-540-25753-0 (print) · 978-3-540-29273-9 (eBook).
- **Klugman, Panjer, Willmot** — *Loss Models: From Data to Decisions*.
  Wiley, 5th edition, 2019.

## Comonads and contextual computation

- **Uustalu, Vene** — *Comonadic Notions of Computation*. Electronic
  Notes in Theoretical Computer Science 203(5):263–284, 2008.
